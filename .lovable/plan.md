# Personal apology website

## What I’ll build
- Replace the blank page with one intimate, scrollable apology story.
- Start with the requested opening and reveal the remaining sections only after “Fine, →”.
- Present the apology as a clean editorial letter with a handwritten signature.
- Add two clickable Polaroid-style evidence cards with short, editable explanations.
- Integrate the uploaded song in a custom story-focused player with play/pause, progress, and time controls; it will never autoplay.
- Finish with both answer choices, subtle confetti for “Yes,” and the requested respectful response for “I’m still mad.”

## Visual direction
- Premium cream paper palette with restrained rose-red accents, fine rules, and tactile editorial details.
- Modern sans-serif typography paired with a sparingly used handwritten font.
- Mobile-first spacing, smooth scrolling, and quiet reveal/press animations with reduced-motion support.
- No template-like hearts, glitter, gradients, manipulative buttons, or excessive decoration.

## Technical details
- Keep all personal copy and song labels together in one editable content object.
- Use the uploaded audio as a managed project asset.
- Keep everything local and frontend-only, with no accounts, database, APIs, or paid services.
- Add page-specific title and sharing metadata, then verify the key interactions on desktop and mobile.
